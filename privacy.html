Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what the Run Fun app ("Run Fun", "we") collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Run Fun is developed by Space Out (Shahmeer Alamtab). Questions: shahmeer.alamtab@gmail.com.

Run Fun does not track your location in the background when you are not running. While a run is being recorded on Android, a notification is shown for the whole time. We do not use advertising, ad identifiers or third-party analytics, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage (data is encrypted in transit and at rest)
  • Google: "Sign in with Google" and map display on Android (Google Maps)
  • Apple: "Sign in with Apple" and map display on iPhone (Apple Maps)
  • Expo: app builds and updates

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, photo, runs, friends, messages, voice notes and any lobbies you host, immediately. See Delete your account for other ways to request deletion.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Database access rules ensure users can only read data they are allowed to see (for example, only members of a lobby can see its live map and chat).

Children

Run Fun is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
