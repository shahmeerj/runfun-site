Delete your Run Fun account

Run Fun by Space Out

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Run Fun and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email shahmeer.alamtab@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Run Fun account". We will delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Everything linked to your account: email and sign-in, profile (name, username, photo), all runs and routes, statistics, friends, lobby memberships, live positions, chat messages, voice notes, blocks, and any lobbies you host. Reports you filed about other runners are kept without your name attached.

What is kept

Nothing. We do not keep backups of deleted accounts beyond our hosting provider's routine database backups, which are overwritten within 7 days.
